Goal planning calculation rules
- Future cost is calculated using compound inflation.
- This project uses an explicit 6% annual inflation rate: Future Cost = Current Cost × (1 + 0.06)^Years.
- Monthly investment uses the future-value-of-monthly-contributions formula.
- Expected annual return assumptions are 6% short-term, 8% medium-term and 10% long-term.
- Marriage, Car and Home are calculated separately because their timelines can differ.
